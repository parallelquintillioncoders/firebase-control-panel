Privacy & local storage

PQC Launchpad by Parallel Quintillion Coders

Essential storage

Your consent choice, theme, display mode and language preferences are stored in this browser. If your dashboard requires a password, the local server uses an essential session cookie to keep you signed in. Declining optional features does not disable authentication.

Optional external fonts

Accepting loads fonts from Google Fonts. Your browser connects to Google, which receives request information including your IP address. Declining uses fonts already on your device. Changing to decline stops future font loads; it cannot undo requests already made.

Your workspace

Builds and deployment history remain on your local server. Firebase account access and deployments communicate with Google through the existing Firebase CLI. This panel does not add analytics or advertising tracking.

Change your choice at any time using Cookie preferences in the footer.
